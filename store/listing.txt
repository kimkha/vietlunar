ENGLISH LISTING

Name:
VietLunar - Lunar Calendar for Vietnam

Short description (max 132 chars):
Vietnamese lunar calendar on your toolbar, with solar and lunar dates in one monthly view.

Detailed description:
VietLunar shows the Vietnamese lunar date on the Chrome toolbar and opens a monthly calendar with both solar (Dương lịch) and lunar (Âm lịch) dates.

What you get
• Toolbar icon with today’s lunar day number
• Monthly calendar: solar date + lunar date in each cell
• Click a day for Can Chi, tiết khí, and giờ hoàng đạo
• Works offline — no account, no tracking, no website access
• English and Vietnamese UI strings

This update (0.4.0) migrates the extension to Manifest V3 so it stays compatible with current Chrome.

Calendar calculations are based on Hồ Ngọc Đức’s âm lịch algorithm.

Category: Productivity
Language: English (default), Vietnamese

Mô tả ngắn:
