VietLunar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

VietLunar (Chrome extension) is a Vietnamese lunar calendar. It shows today’s lunar day on the toolbar and a monthly solar/lunar calendar in the popup.

Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. It does not use analytics, advertising, accounts, or remote servers.

Permissions

The extension uses the Chrome alarms permission only to refresh the toolbar icon about once an hour. Date calculations run locally on your device.
