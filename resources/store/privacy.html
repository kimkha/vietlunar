VietLunar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

VietLunar (Chrome extension) is a Vietnamese lunar calendar. It shows today’s lunar day on the toolbar and a monthly solar/lunar calendar in the popup.

Data we collect

None. Lunar dates you add under “Ngày cần nhớ” stay in Chrome storage on your profile (synced if Chrome Sync is on). The extension does not transmit that data, and does not use analytics, advertising, accounts, or remote servers.

Permissions

The extension uses alarms to refresh the toolbar icon about once an hour, and storage to keep the lunar dates you add. Date calculations run locally on your device.
